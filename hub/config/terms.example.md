# Terms of use

*Replace this with your own terms, and save it as `terms.md` in the server's data folder
(or pass `--terms FILE`). It is shown at `/terms`.*

1. **Accounts.** You are responsible for what is shared from your account. Keep your
   password and app tokens to yourself.
2. **What you share.** Share only what you have the right to share. Public saves and
   classifiers are looked at by an admin before they are listed; admins may remove
   anything, and close accounts, that break these terms.
3. **Private and unlisted.** Private things are visible to you alone; unlisted ones to
   anyone holding the link. Both still count towards your quota.
4. **Service.** The service is provided as it is, without warranty. Keep your own copies
   of what matters to you (any save can be downloaded).
5. **Contact.** Questions go to whoever runs this server (see `/about`).
