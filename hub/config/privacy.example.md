# Privacy

*Replace this with your own note, and save it as `privacy.md` in the server's data
folder (or pass `--privacy FILE`). It is shown at `/privacy`.*

- **What is kept:** your name, a hash of your password, an email address if you give one
  (only admins see it; nothing is ever sent to it), what you write on your page, and the
  saves and classifiers you keep or share here.
- **Sessions and tokens** are kept only as hashes. Network addresses are not stored; the
  rate limits hold a hash of an address in memory for at most an hour.
- **Logs** record each request's method, path, status and time: no addresses, names,
  query strings or secrets.
- **Your saves** in the web app are yours alone; no other member can open them.
- **Leaving:** ask an admin to remove your account and everything you shared.
